Root is the notched treasury seal; Research owns the top branch band; Data inherits a second band and Scraper terminates it; Analysis is a lower fork. Operations has its own separate lower sleeve. Arena uses the integrated mission (candidate A). A large notched bone mission occupies the central field; funding resolves from Root above, submission lanes approach from the left, the verifier occupies the right notch, and the real settled receipt appears beyond that threshold. Existing lifecycle controls belong inside the mission. Metadata and actual evidence remain below as quiet readable rows. A won over the top-led alternative because the mission, decision and outcome become one asymmetrical object rather than a process diagram or broad secondary control slab. The selected unpaid result remains copper; only the existing settlement event permits a route and receipt beyond the verifier.
