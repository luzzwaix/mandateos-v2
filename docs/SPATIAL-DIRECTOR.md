## The direction: The Authority Loom

MandateOS is one traversable object: a monumental, offset system of open structural frames joined by a capital conduit. The Root is its fixed anchor. Delegation adds a chamber at the end of an active rail; recursive delegation pushes the structure farther into depth. A revoke severs the rail at its ancestor and leaves the physical structure visible but unpowered. An unrelated sibling stays lit. The silhouette should read as engineered financial infrastructure from across the room—not as boxes on a graph, a literal tree, or an abstract crypto sculpture.

The Loom is not a decorative scene placed behind the app. It is the organization itself. Its branch geometry and state come from the same authority data used by Command. The Story world may make that object spatial; Command presents its identical organization as a precise, flat operating instrument; Arena turns the same capital rail into an execution lane.

### What a judge should remember

Mint light travels from the Root through Research, Data, and Scraper. On revoke, a single red cut appears at Research and authority goes dark downstream in three clean beats. The rooms remain. Operations stays lit. The image explains propagation, ancestry, selective revocation, and survival without a paragraph.

## Reference deconstruction

These are three useful, specific craft references—not three skins to imitate. The live Trionn and OFF+BRAND pages did not render reliably in the inspected browser session; their published case studies remain available, so this document separates observed page behavior from documented technique.

### TRIONN — authored motion as one system

**Observed / documented:** The current site presents itself as an independent digital studio and leads with “14+ years shaping digital direction.” Its published technical case study describes a WebGL hero symbol built from separately animated panels; hover, scroll, and hold-to-blast all feed the same `explodeAmt` state. The headline remains accessible DOM and is synchronized with the canvas. The article also documents section-specific camera and interaction systems rather than one ambient effect applied everywhere. The live browser session returned a load error, so this is a teardown of the published build and case study, not a claim that the current live hero was visually captured.

- **What makes it expensive:** The 3D object, readable headline, scroll, and interaction resolve as one authored composition. The model is not a second layer with unrelated animation.
- **What makes it memorable:** A person can physically alter one coherent object—separate it, then bring it back together.
- **If the logo disappeared:** The reveal, fracture, and reassembly would still communicate intent through timing and form.
- **Transform for MandateOS:** A mandate state drives chamber construction and the capital rail together. Revoke uses the same continuity in reverse. Take the shared-state choreography, not Trionn’s symbol, blast effect, or studio branding.

Sources: [TRIONN](https://trionn.com/) · [TRIONN technical case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/15/the-architecture-behind-trionn-coordinating-gsap-three-js-lenis-and-web-audio/)

### Cerebrium — large type against product proof

**Observed / documented:** In the live homepage capture, a very large, light-weight statement occupies the lower-left of a dark navy field; one word carries a vivid accent. A concise explanation and two actions sit far to the right. The deliberate distance between the headline and supporting copy makes the first viewport feel composed instead of tiled. Lower on the live page, measured comparisons, a region/capacity view, and a real terminal excerpt turn the claim into product evidence. Its published case study describes multiple environments tied together by animated paths. The paths are static meshes whose moving UV masks create pulses; paths reveal with camera distance. The team describes migrating away from WebGPU when shader initialization threatened to take close to twenty seconds.

- **What makes it expensive:** Editorial scale is paired with proof that has specific content and a clear job. The canvas and DOM do different work but share the same story.
- **What makes it memorable:** Infrastructure that is normally invisible is given a perceptible path and scale.
- **If the logo disappeared:** The scale headline, moving routes, comparative measurements, and terminal evidence would still identify the product’s promise.
- **Transform for MandateOS:** Make capital physically legible as a rail, and make authority legible as the permission to pass through successive structural thresholds. Use the static-path / moving-mask technique for one controlled signal. Do not borrow Cerebrium’s palette, cloud map, or generic network imagery.

Sources: [Cerebrium](https://cerebrium.ai/) · [Cerebrium technical case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/07/23/building-cerebrium-making-serverless-infrastructure-tangible/)

## Why the current build reads cheaper

The current frontend explains the product, but its visual language undersells the mechanism.

1. **No dominant silhouette.** The eye lands on a headline, then searches the thin diagram. The organization is information inside the page instead of the page’s memorable object.
2. **Depth is mostly implied by outlines.** Skewed boundaries and connecting lines do not establish foreground, midground, rear structure, or a camera position.
3. **The capital is numerically present but physically absent.** Amounts sit inside small plaques; there is no continuous rail, change in mass, or visible transfer of authority.
4. **Repeated node rectangles set the visual tone.** Their card anatomy says admin dashboard before the viewer can understand recursive authority.
5. **Story changes sections instead of transforming a world.** Text / diagram / text / diagram gives each chapter a separate composition. Nothing accumulates in the visitor’s visual memory.
6. **The key event stays a diagram.** The copper outline marks impact, but it does not feel like a volume closing around one branch. The revoke is a state label rather than an event in space.
7. **Typography carries every scene at roughly the same distance.** Headlines are clear but do not make a cinematic scale contrast with the organization object.
8. **Arena is a sequence drawing.** Capital source → mission → verifier → payout is correct semantics, but currently lacks arrival, competition, a decision gate, and resolution.
9. **The screen contains useful information but little art direction.** There is no designed lighting, material, intentional camera, or authored custom asset to make MandateOS recognizable without its wordmark.
10. **Previous design advice stopped before a visual target.** A reference list, motion checklist, or UX audit cannot resolve object silhouette and composition. Those need approval in actual frames and browser comparison against those frames.

## Home experience: five composed scenes

### 01 — Arrival

Show the full line—**DON’T GIVE AGENTS WALLETS. GIVE THEM MANDATES.**—at editorial scale, deliberately broken into a few short lines. The Root and first section of the Loom fill the right side and extend beyond the frame. The CTA is part of the scene’s control system: **Delegate Research**. A visible capital figure is attached to the root rail; do not put the story in a centered SaaS hero column. The initial camera is close enough that the Loom’s far end is not yet known.

### 02 — Authority expands

User action starts the sequence. Light travels before geometry. At its endpoint, the Research chamber locks into place; the capital amount resolves with it. Research’s own rail then constructs Data and Analysis; Data constructs Scraper. Operations appears as a stable sibling off Root, never as part of the Research branch. The object grows through authored parallax and camera pull-back, not a cloud of particles or six tiles fading in.

### 03 — Trace / control

Selecting Scraper shifts focus to the descendant while keeping Root in frame. Its complete path back to Root brightens in sequence. Unrelated Operations recedes in contrast but remains visible. Keep the camera move small enough that the visitor never loses the source of authority.

### 04 — Blast Radius / Cascade

Preview draws a copper spatial boundary around the Research substructure; all nodes still say ACTIVE. Confirm then puts a red interruption at Research. A short downward response turns Data, Analysis, and Scraper rails dark in order. The remaining physical structure and Operations stay visible. A separate financial line describes the actual program’s capital consequence; no animated money returns to Root unless the transaction semantics confirm a recovery.

### 05 — Enter Command

The camera moves to an orthographic view and presses the Loom into a flat operational tree. The selected Scraper path and its hierarchy remain at the same coordinates as far as possible. The real Command inspector appears in bone. The route change should feel like moving to the operating face of the object, not loading a new dashboard.

## Command visual language

- **View:** flat and orthographic. No WebGL scene behind the operating surface, no ambient glow, no faux-depth shadows.
- **Tree:** largest region. Thin, exact relation rails; stronger line weight only on selected path. Nodes are labels with tightly composed amount/state notation, not repeated cards.
- **Selection:** click/tap a mandate to lock the inspector and highlight its full authority path. Hover previews only; it must not mutate selection or product state.
- **Inspector:** bone financial instrument with clear local/effective authority, available amount, subtree amount, depth/children, parent, complete Authority Path, and applicable actions. Never substitute gray skeleton bars.
- **Money:** align decimals; use mono for amounts/IDs/timestamps. Communicate mandate capacity in the structure with a narrow rail segment or restrained proportional thickness, not a generic progress bar.
- **Evidence:** actual readable activity and transaction state. Demo uses an explicit “DEMO · SIMULATED · NO TRANSACTION” label. Devnet shows only wallet/program-confirmed state and the real signature, if one exists. No fake explorer URL or signature.
- **Blast Radius:** an explicit analysis mode in the working tree. Copper line surrounds affected descendants; unaffected siblings remain in the same view. Preview never changes active state.
- **Cascade:** show the recorded transaction/program outcome and the visual authority path break as distinct facts. Do not make account balance or reclaim amounts up from animation alone.
- **Interaction polish:** keyboard-selectable nodes, visible focus ring, inspector updates without layout jump, numeric change animation only for confirmed state deltas. Avoid generic chart motion and always-on pulsing.

## Arena visual language

Arena remains connected to the Loom through a shared capital rail, but the room opens into a wider shallow-spatial execution stage. The mission is one solid focal instrument. Three or fewer submission lanes can converge on it; a verifier gate visibly constrains which lane continues. Selected result receives the only warm settlement color. Keep mission ID, bounty, verifier status, winner, payout state, and evidence in real DOM text attached to the stage. No avatars, collectible tokens, podium, or card marketplace.

## Art direction

### Composition and camera

- **Story:** 1440×900; 36–40% editorial text field, 60–64% Authority Loom. Object is asymmetrically weighted and may crop at the frame edges. Initial 30–35mm three-quarter camera; widen gradually to show full branching hierarchy. No 360° spins.
- **Command:** near-orthographic, flat, left-weighted tree and right inspector. Framing is chosen for legibility, not spectacle.
- **Arena:** 30–35mm shallow three-quarter stage. Mission is the focal mass; verifier gate is an offset visual hinge; evidence sits in a separate quiet rail.
- **Typography:** one bold condensed display cut for Story at roughly 88–116 px at 1440 width; operational sans around 14–16 px; mono 11–13 px for labels, amounts, IDs, states, and evidence. Keep body statements short. Use the installed font family where it fits; add at most one display font after confirming licensing and rendering.

### Materials, light, and color

- **World:** layered graphite planes, with slight cool/warm variation and broad falloff; never one flat #111 field.
- **Loom:** dark smoked metal / graphite ceramic with fine edge response. Build shape from overlapping structural frames and apertures—not rounded glass panels or thick beveled cards.
- **Financial surface:** flat bone, reserved for inspector, exact values, and critical evidence.
- **Key light:** one broad cool source from the upper rear left; mint is emitted only by active authority. Narrow highlights reveal silhouette; no scene-wide bloom.
- **Mint:** active/effective authority and currently available active rail.
- **Copper:** preview boundary and pending consequence only.
- **Red:** confirmed broken/invalid authority path only.
- **Brass:** verified settlement only, if it improves outcome clarity.
- **Texture:** barely perceptible fine material variation to stop large surfaces reading as vector fills. No noisy grain overlay, starfield, smoke cloud, random particle drift, or generic purple gradient.

### Signature visual effects

1. **Path pulse:** one narrow UV-masked pulse travels along the capital/authority rail after an actual demo or confirmed state transition. It is not on a loop at idle.
2. **Chamber construction:** geometry opens/extrudes from the active endpoint; the authority path lights first, chamber resolves second, amount locks last.
3. **Impact volume:** copper lines construct a spatial, branch-shaped enclosure in preview. Nothing inside changes status until confirmation.
4. **Cascade interruption:** red cut marker at the revoked ancestor, then ordered light loss downward. Structural geometry remains.
5. **Matched plane change:** camera / Loom depth compresses into the flat Command tree without a blank transition frame.

No sound is required. If later added, it must be opt-in, low, short, and tied only to delegate, revoke, or settlement; the visual product must remain fully understandable with sound muted.

## Implemented mechanics

The same keyed native tree survives every Story chapter. Projection encodes hierarchy depth `z = −0.55 × depth`, never capital. Names, balances and actions stay flat DOM. Thin SVG rims, connector bends, and the prism use the same coordinates. Spacious Story fields use projected depth; smaller fields use the flat recipe to keep the Research enclosure clear of Operations. Reduced motion is flat.

- Genesis: mint connector stroke grows from 80–360 ms; child clip/6 px endpoint settle starts at 360 ms and lasts 240 ms. Desktop grant cadence is 720 ms. Mobile uses a 220 ms ledger lineage stroke followed by 100 ms label resolution, with a 360 ms cadence. Reduced motion constructs immediately.
- Recursion: deterministic coordinates remain stable through construction. Research → Data → Scraper deepen; Analysis shares Data's level and Operations keeps independent Root authority. Existing creation/accounting order remains unchanged.
- Trace: reverse SVG traversal has 70 ms ancestor staggering. Spacious Story gets one restrained 10 px/−8 px focus shift over 450 ms. Root and Operations remain readable. Command gets no camera motion.
- Blast Radius: twelve copper wires enclose Research/Data/Analysis/Scraper with label padding. Front constructs for 220 ms, rear/depth connections for the next 160 ms. Cancel uses a bounded 180 ms Web Animation and removes only the decorative wire copy. Every account stays active.
- Cascade: authoritative local/effective labels and exact balances update immediately. Research cut/notch resolves over 90 ms. Downstream structural channels become neutral and correctly dashed; Data/Analysis rims lose authority at 220–340 ms, Scraper at 340–460 ms. Rear volume retracts at 460–620 ms. Rejection traversal starts only when the user invokes the existing simulated blocked spend. Operations geometry, text, opacity and authority are unchanged.
- Plane change: one 520 ms imperative projection clock interpolates the existing labels/paths to Command's actual graph recipe: 144 px rail above the 1280 px breakpoint, existing 336/320 px inspector, map origin at y=176. Native controls stay mounted. Route navigation occurs at 680 ms. All six final label rectangles match mounted Command within 1 px. Cleanup restores labels, paths and rims if interrupted.
- Arena: existing mission plane remains 10°. Submission backplanes use 0/12/24 px Z and 8° perspective while labels stay flat. Existing 420 ms funding, 240 ms submission, 320 ms verifier gate and 480 ms settled payout mechanics remain tied to their existing events. Verification and payout retain one atomic receipt.

The checkout has CSS/Web Animations motion machinery, with no installed `motion` or `framer-motion` package. That existing machinery was retained; no second animation runtime was added. The flatten clock does no per-frame React state update and no frame callback changes account state. A settled browser probe recorded **zero app-scheduled rAF calls over 1.2 seconds**.

Source mechanics were studied, not copied as UI templates: [R3F demand rendering](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance), [R3F mutation/resource guidance](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/pitfalls), [Drei label projection](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/misc/html), and [Motion layout continuity](https://motion.dev/docs/react-layout-animations).
